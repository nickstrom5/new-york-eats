# Leads: Central NY, Southern Tier east, North Country

Places that probably belong in a guide but could not be fully verified in this pass. "DOH" = NYS Health Dept food-service
inspection open data (health.data.ny.gov, dataset cnih-y5dw); "AgM" = NYS Agriculture & Markets retail food inspections
(data.ny.gov, dataset d6dy-3h7r). Both are dated official listings and were used as open-status evidence in the guide file.

  articles on its riggies (opened July 1991, Riggie Cup wins) returned 429 twice, so no readable dish source. Settle: read
  romesentinel.com "Despite challenges, Teddy's, in Rome, carries on" or a current menu.

  riggies or greens. Settle: own menu.
- **Georgio's Village Cafe**, 62 Genesee St, New Hartford 13413. Named for Utica greens by I LOVE NY (2024), but its site is stale
  (copyright 2022), there is no DOH row, and DOH shows a different restaurant at 60 Genesee St. Possibly closed. Settle: a
  2025-26 news item or an active menu.
- **Babe's at Harbor Point**, 80 N Genesee St, Utica 13501. DOH 2026-03 shows it open; no source for riggies or greens.
- **Charlie's Pizza**, 56 Kellogg Rd, New Hartford. DOH 2026-04 open; a 2016 blog lists a "Charlie's Pizza" for Utica greens,
  but it's unclear it is this one.
- **Iconic Restaurant by Chesterfield**, inside the DoubleTree, 102 Lafayette St, Utica. Sister to Chesterfield's Tavolo
  (Rome Sentinel, Feb 2026); dishes not confirmed.
- **Delmonico's Italian Steakhouse** (Utica, Syracuse). Chain site lists both cities but no addresses or menus readable; no DOH row
  found for Utica.

- **Chesterfield's Tavolo riggies**: the guide record carries only `greens`. The Toast menu that lists Chicken Riggies is 403;

## oldest
- **Springside Inn**, 6141 W Lake Rd, Auburn (Fleming). Open (DOH 2026-08; Thanksgiving 2026 dinner on its site). Its own history:
  built 1851 as a boys' school, a summer resort from 1919, a year-round hotel and restaurant by the 1940s. Left out because food
  service before 1920 isn't shown. Settle: a source on dining at the 1919 resort.
- **Aurora Inn (1833 Kitchen & Bar)**, 378 Main St, Aurora (Cayuga). Site active (copyright 2026), but no DOH row and no readable
  history of continuous operation (it was restored and reopened in the 2000s). Settle: Inns of Aurora history page or Wikipedia.
- **The Woods Inn**, 148 Route 28, Inlet (Hamilton). DOH 2026-08 open; 2025 Inlet business association member. Build year
  (1890s?) unsourced.
- **Adirondack Hotel**, 1245 Main St, Long Lake (Hamilton). DOH 2026-08 open; own site returned 403; build year unsourced.
- **Ferlo's Original Bakery**, Rome: "over 120 years in business" (Rome Sentinel 2025), but not shown at 522 Floyd Ave that long.
- **Hemstrought's Bakery**, Utica: dates to 1920, but not shown at 900 Oswego St since then.
- **O'Scugnizzo Pizzeria**, Utica: 1914 business, but at 614 Bleecker St only since the mid-1960s, so not `oldest`.
- **Heid's of Liverpool**: hot dog stand from 1917, but at 305 Oswego St only since 1930, so not `oldest`.
- **Moretti's** (Elmira, 1917 claim) and **Thousand Islands Inn** (Clayton, 1897 claim): no DOH rows; status unknown.
- **Hotel Utica** (1912): now a DoubleTree; no source on continuous restaurant service.
