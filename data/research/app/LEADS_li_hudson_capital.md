A search snippet says 633 Jericho Tpke. umbertosnhp.com is a parked ad domain, and umbertos.com belongs to an unrelated South Carolina restaurant. *Settle:* any readable page with the address, such as a Village of New Hyde Park page or an official listing.

## oldest
- **Jack's Oyster House**, 44 State St, Albany. It's open (site © 2026, events in Oct 2026, now owned by NeoVista Equities). It dates to 1913, but search snippets say it opened at Beaver and Green streets and moved to State St in 1937, so it fails the rule that the place must date to before 1920 at this address. It also closed in 2022 and later reopened. Left out on purpose.
- **Olde Bryan Inn**, 123 Maple Ave, Saratoga Springs. Its own site returned 403. It claims 1773, but snippets say the stone building dates to 1832 and the restaurant to 1979, so it probably fails the rule.
- **The 1770 House**, 143 Main St, East Hampton. It's open (site updated Feb 2026, news posts from Dec 2025). The building dates to 1770. Time Out (2017) says it became an inn in 1770, but the inn's own history describes a store in 1795 and a later inn era. *Settle:* when it first served food or drink as an inn or tavern.
- **Three Village Inn / Mirabelle Restaurant & Tavern**, 150 Main St, Stony Brook. It's a colonial-era house but not a colonial inn. Check its inn and restaurant start year.
- **Milleridge Inn**, Jericho. It's a 1672 house, but it has been a restaurant only since the 1930s, so it fails the rule unless it served food earlier. Newsday family coverage from 2025–26 shows it active.
- **North Plank Road Tavern**, 30 Plank Rd, Newburgh ("in operation since 1801"). Its own site says it is temporarily closed, with daily dinner suspended and private events only.
- **Old Drovers Inn** (Dover Plains), **The Rhinecliff** (Rhinecliff) and **The American Hotel** (Sag Harbor). All three sites returned 403.
- **Kittle House** (Chappaqua) and **DePuy Canal House** (High Falls). Both domains now serve casino spam pages, so their status is unknown and they may be closed. *Settle:* local news.
- **The Chequit** (Shelter Island Heights). Its domain is parked or for sale, so its status is unknown.
- **Old Mill Inn** (Mattituck; a tavern since 1903, per a search snippet). Its site timed out, and the Newsday URL returned 404.
- **Bird & Bottle Inn** (Garrison, 1761). It has a Wix site with no readable text. **Baird's Tavern** (Warwick, 1766, reopened 2017) and **Iron Forge Inn** (Warwick) have no working site.
- **Hoffman House** (Kingston). The building is from the 1600s, but it has been a restaurant only since the 1970s, so it fails the rule.
