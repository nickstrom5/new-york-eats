# Manhattan leads (not yet in `manhattan.json`)

Places that probably belong in a guide but are missing a readable 2025–26 source, a confirmed address, or a fact the kind depends on.
"DOHMH" = NYC Health Department restaurant-inspection open data
(`https://data.cityofnewyork.us/resource/43nn-pn8j.json?camis=<id>`), which gives a dated city record for food-service places
(it doesn't cover retail-only bakeries or appetizing stores; those are in the NYS Ag & Markets license list, `data.ny.gov` dataset `9a8c-vfzj`).

## slice
- **Upside Pizza (original), Garment District**: Time Out (2019) gives 598 8th Ave, but DOHMH has no record there. It does list Upside at 270 W 39th St (10018, 2026-02), probably the same corner, plus 51 Spring St, 20 E 40th St, 555 6th Ave, 2878 Broadway, 1231 Broadway and 63 Wall St. Hoodline (Jun 2026) says the first shop opened 2019. *To settle:* the street address of the original shop (the brand's locations page 404'd).
- **2 Bros Pizza, 32 St Marks Pl (10003)**: DOHMH 41334807, 2026-01. *To settle:* a source for its history or opening year (the site has no history or addresses).
- **Patsy's Pizzeria, East Harlem (DOHMH: 2289 1st Ave, 10035, 2026-04)**: the 1933 original. *To settle:* a source showing it sells slices at the counter, and the street number (2287 vs 2289).
- **Sofia Pizza Shoppe, 989 1st Ave (10022)**: DOHMH 50124268, 2026-03; founded 2016 (own site). Only a 2024 article calls it a slice shop. *To settle:* a 2025–26 source on its slices.
- **Unregular Pizza, 135 4th Ave and 88 Essex St**: DOHMH 2026. It sells Roman-style squares, so it may not fit `slice`. *To settle:* an editorial call on the kind.
- **Denino's (93 MacDougal St, DOHMH 2026-02) / Joe & Pat's (168 1st Ave, DOHMH 2025-11)**: Manhattan branches of Staten Island pizzerias, both in the Time Out pizza list (Oct 2025). Probably whole pies, not slices. *To settle:* whether they sell by the slice.
- **Lombardi's, 32 Spring St (DOHMH 2026-04)**: coal-oven whole pies, not a slice shop. The 1905 date is for the original across the street, so it isn't `oldest` at this address either.
- Rubirosa (235 Mulberry St) left out on purpose: it's a sit-down restaurant, not a slice counter.

## oldest
- **Yonah Schimmel's Knish Bakery, 137 E Houston St (10002)**: open in 2026 (own site, DOHMH 2026-03). It has sold knishes since 1890, but per Wikipedia it moved to this side of Houston St in the 1930s, so it isn't pre-1920 at this address. No other kind fits a knish bakery. *To settle:* whether the app wants it under a different kind.
- **Vincent's, 119 Mott St (10013)**: DOHMH 2025-10, plus a new permit pending. Claims 1904, but the only site found is for its Long Island branch. *To settle:* a source for the 1904 claim at this corner.
- **Mulberry Street Bar, 176½ Mulberry St (DOHMH 2026-09)**: often described as a very old bar. *To settle:* a source for its opening year at this address.
- **Katz's Delicatessen**: in the guide as `deli` only. It was founded in 1888 across Ludlow St and moved to this corner during subway construction. Wikipedia puts the move in the 1930s; 6sqft (2023) says 1917. *To settle:* the year of the move. If it was 1917, add `oldest`.
- **Russ & Daughters**: at 179 E Houston St since 1920, which misses the pre-1920 cutoff by a year. Not tagged `oldest`.
- **Bridge Cafe, 279 Water St**: no DOHMH record. It has been closed since Hurricane Sandy (2012). *To settle:* any reopening news; otherwise treat as closed.
