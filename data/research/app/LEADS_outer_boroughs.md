# LEADS: Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx, Staten Island

These places probably belong in a guide but couldn't be verified under the README rules. Each entry gives the reason and what would settle it.

**Blocked hosts.** Robots.txt disallows ClaudeBot, Claude-User or anthropic-ai on Eater NY, Grub Street/nymag, SILive, QNS, amNY, Brooklyn Paper, Brownstoner, Queens Chronicle, NY1, NY Post, Daily News, NYT, Patch, The Infatuation, Thrillist, Serious Eats and Food & Wine. Gothamist redirects bots to tollbit (a pay-to-crawl gate), so I treated it as blocked. Forbes article pages returned 403. Most borough-local news is therefore out of reach, and that's the main reason this list is long. Staten Island has no verified entries.

## slice
- **Joe & Pat's Pizzeria And Restaurant**, 1758 Victory Blvd, Staten Island 10314 (since 1960). Time Out's pizza list (Oct 23, 2025) shows it open and says Staten Islanders go there "for a good slice". It also has thin-crust pies and a Tri Pie. Neither the own site (joeandpatsny.com) nor its menu page lists a single-slice item. *Settle:* a dated menu or a source that says it sells slices at the counter. If it does, add it as `slice`.
- **Denino's Pizzeria & Tavern**, 524 Port Richmond Ave, Staten Island 10302. Time Out (Oct 2025) and Time Out's chefs guide (Sep 1, 2026) show it open. It's a tavern with whole pies (clam pie, M.O.R.), and sources disagree on the founding year: 1937 vs 1951. No `kind` fits unless it sells slices.
- **New Park Pizza**, 156-71 Cross Bay Blvd, Howard Beach 11414 (since 1956, per its own site). Slices are confirmed by Time Out (2011). No 2025–26 source was found: the own site is undated and QNS/Eater are blocked. *Settle:* any dated 2025–26 story or menu.
- **Krispy Pizza**, Dyker Heights (13th Ave). Its own site (krispypizza.com) says it began about 50 years ago in Brooklyn and shows only a © 2026 footer. It gives no street address or slice menu.
- **Rose & Joe's Italian Bakery**, Astoria (31st St). Time Out's chefs guide (Sep 2026) names it, but no fetchable source gave the address or confirmed its Sicilian/grandma squares. roseandjoes.com is an "opening soon" Shopify page and may not be theirs.
- **Full Moon Pizzeria**, 600 E 187th St, Bronx 10458. The newest source is Time Out from 2010.
- **Luigi's Pizza** (South Slope, 50+ years), **La Flor** (Crown Heights), **R. Slice** (Roberta's slice shop, Bushwick) and **Lucia Pizza of Avenue X** (Sheepshead Bay) all come from bkmag's slice-shop roundup, dated Aug 2024. *Settle:* a 2025–26 source plus addresses.
- **L&B Spumoni Gardens DUMBO**, 46 Old Fulton St, 11201. Time Out (Oct 2025) lists it, but I couldn't confirm it runs as a slice counter rather than a sit-down room. It's a possible branch record.
- **Ace's Pizza**, 637 Driggs Ave, Williamsburg. Time Out (Oct 2025) shows it open, but it's Detroit-style, so I left it out of `slice` on purpose.
- **Lenny's Pizza** closed in Feb 2023 (see the closed file). **Totonno's** in Coney Island makes whole pies only and opened in 1924, so it's neither `slice` nor `oldest`. It was for sale per bkmag (Jan 7, 2026).
- **Cross Bronx Pizza** (Castle Hill) came up only in a search snippet and was never read.

## oldest
- **Gargiulo's**, 2911 W 15th St, Coney Island 11224. It was founded in 1907 on Mermaid Ave and later moved; neither its own site nor Time Out dates the move. If the move came before 1920 it qualifies, otherwise it doesn't. The site shows only "© 2017–2026".
- **Killmeyer's Old Bavaria Inn**, 4254 Arthur Kill Rd, Staten Island 10309. 6sqft (2023) says Nicolas Killmeyer bought the property around 1855 and installed a mahogany bar in 1890. The current owners' site says "serving Staten Island since 1967", and its only date is a © 2026 footer. *Settle:* a source for continuous food or drink service at the address before 1920, plus a dated 2025–26 event such as an Oktoberfest date.
- **Teddy's Bar & Grill**, 96 Berry St, Williamsburg. Untapped (modified Aug 2025) says it opened as a tavern in 1887 and now serves a full food menu. No own site was reachable and there's no other 2025–26 source.
- **Bar Ferdinando**, 151 Union St, Carroll Gardens. A new 2026 restaurant and bar in the old Ferdinando's Focacceria space (food served there since 1904). It's a new business that keeps some Ferdinando's dishes, so it isn't the same restaurant. Decide whether `oldest` applies; I think not.
- **Ann & Tony's** (Arthur Ave, 1927) and **Dominick's** (Arthur Ave) don't meet the pre-1920 rule. Listed for awareness only.
