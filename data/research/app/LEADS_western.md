# LEADS: Western New York and the western Finger Lakes

These places probably belong in a guide, but I couldn't verify them to the README standard. Each entry gives the reason and what would settle it.

Hosts I skipped because robots.txt blocks Claude agents or the site returned a 403: Buffalo News, Buffalo Spree, Democrat & Chronicle (and its AOL copies), 13WHAM, WHEC, WROC/RochesterFirst, WIVB, Spectrum News, CITY/roccitymag.com (403), WGRZ (403), tourchautauqua.com, parks.ny.gov (Historic Business Registry), Livingston County News, Finger Lakes Times, Eater, Serious Eats, newyorkupstate.com and syracuse.com. Many Rochester and Buffalo food stories are behind these sources.

## beef on weck
- **Swiston's Beef & Keg**, 101 Young St, Tonawanda (city) 14150. Family-owned since 1983, and the menu is only beef on weck and chili. The only current evidence is an auto-synced Visit Buffalo listing; swistonsbeefandkeg.com timed out. To settle: a 2025-26 news item or a dated menu.
- **Eckl's @ Larkin**, 703 Seneca St, Buffalo. Search snippets say the original Orchard Park Eckl's (4936 Ellicott Rd) closed after 86 years, and that the Larkin steakhouse reopened after a pandemic shutdown. Both sources are WGRZ and WIVB, which I couldn't read. To settle: a readable 2025-26 source on Larkin's status. Then add the Orchard Park closure to closed_western.json.
- **Nick Charlap's**, West Seneca. A WKBW video headline (Sept 2026) says it is closing and looking for a new location. It's in closed_western.json with no address. To settle: the new location, and whether beef on weck is on its menu.
- **Ulrich's 1868 Tavern** (in the guide as `oldest`). A 2024 Visit Buffalo page says it serves beef on weck. To settle: a current menu before adding the `beef on weck` kind.
- **Duff's** (in the guide as `wings`). It serves beef on weck, per its own site. You could add the kind if you want weck coverage to include wing joints.
- Not researched: Sophia's, the Charlie the Butcher express stands.

## hots / plate

- **Schaller's**, 965 Edgemere Dr, Greece (opened 1956), and 559 E Ridge Rd (1959). Visit Rochester's listing gives Edgemere with ZIP 14621, which looks wrong; Edgemere Dr in Greece is usually 14612. A search snippet (D&C via AOL, not readable) says the Brighton location is closing. To settle: the ZIP and a dated source for each location.
- **The Original Charbroil House**, 1395 Island Cottage Rd, Rochester 14612 (Visit Rochester listing). To settle: any dated source.
- **Westridge Hots** and **Vasko's on Park** (266 Park Ave, 14607). Visit Rochester's Aug 2026 plate guide names both. Westridge has no address yet, and Vasko's is a bar, not a hots shop.
- **Bill Gray's** (many locations; 1938 roots in Webster). Visit Rochester names it for plates, and its 2025 gluten-free menu PDF is dated. It's a chain whose original Ridge Rd stand is gone. Add a flagship only if wanted.
- **Vic & Irv's** (Sea Breeze, Irondequoit; seasonal hots and plates). I found no readable source.
- **Abbott's Frozen Custard** (Rochester, since 1902) is a custard stand, not a guide kind. Rochester Red Wings "Plates" nights are not a place.
- **Buffalo hot dog stands**: Louie's Original Foot Long Hot Dogs, Connors Hot Dog Stand (8905 Lake Shore Rd, Angola; seasonal), George's Hot Dogs (5808 Herman Hill Rd, Boston/Hamburg), Old Man River (375 Niagara St, Tonawanda), Frank Gourmet Hot Dogs. - **Olean / Salamanca Texas hots**. I found none in readable 2025-26 sources.

## oldest (pre-1920 at this address)
- **Roycroft Inn**, East Aurora. The inn opened in 1905 on the Roycroft campus. Douglas Jemal sold it, with the Mansion on Delaware, to Visions Hotels in July 2026 (WKBW). To settle: whether its dining room is open under the new owner, and when food service began.
- **Wallenwein's Hotel**, 641 Oakwood Ave, East Aurora 14052. Visit Buffalo's 2025 food trail calls it an "1882 hotel-turned-restaurant" known for fish fry. To settle: whether food or drink has been served here since before 1920.
- **Richardson's Canal House**, 1474 Marsh Rd, Pittsford. A search snippet calls it an 1818 Erie Canal tavern, restored as a restaurant in 1978. Its current status is unknown. To settle: whether it is open in 2025-26, and whether it served continuously since before 1920 (probably not).
- **Lewiston Frontier House** (1824). WKBW reported in June 2025 that it would open as a restaurant with apartments. Service isn't continuous, so it fails the README test unless the README makes an exception for historic inns.
- **Holloway House**, East Bloomfield (1808 tavern; seasonal). It has been a restaurant since about 1960, which makes it a historic inn rather than one serving since before 1920. Not checked.
- **Swannie House** (Buffalo), **Avon Inn** (Livingston), **Big Tree Inn** (Geneseo). Not checked.
- **Near misses**, not pre-1920 at this address. Schwabl's has been here since 1942 (it claims 1837). Santasiero's dates from the 1920s (reopened June 2026 under new owners, per WKBW). Chef's dates from 1923. Texas Hot dates from 1921. Nick Tahou's 1918 date belongs to an earlier building next door.
