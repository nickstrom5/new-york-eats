Brooklyn at a glance

Our directory counts 8,117 places to eat and drink in Brooklyn, in neighborhoods from Williamsburg, Park Slope, and Downtown Brooklyn-DUMBO-Boerum Hill on. Coffee & Café (1,023), Bar & Pub (815), and Bakery & Sweets (710) lead the kinds of food. 2,106 hold an on-premises liquor license from the State Liquor Authority. 976 are locations of a chain with five or more in New York. 23 are on our hand-checked guides; the longest at the same address is Peter Luger Steak House, there since 1887.

Health inspections here

6,273 places in Brooklyn have a letter grade or grade status on file with the NYC Health Department: 5,434 A, 418 B and 124 C, and 297 pending or not yet graded. Each grade is official and dated, from the Health Department's records through Sep 29, 2026; we never compute one from an inspection score. In the web search, each place's panel shows its grade, its latest inspection and a link to the Health Department's full history.

About the inspection results

NYC letter grades are the NYC Health Department's own, labeled official and shown with the date of the graded inspection: A, B or C, posted at the door. N means not yet graded, Z grade pending, and P grade pending on re-opening after a closure. We never compute a grade from an inspection score. Source: NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene, NYC Open Data, records through Sep 29, 2026; the full history is on the Health Department's ABCEats site.
