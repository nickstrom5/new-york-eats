Manhattan at a glance

Our directory counts 12,069 places to eat and drink in Manhattan, in neighborhoods from Midtown-Times Square, Midtown South-Flatiron-Union Square, and East Village on. Coffee & Café (1,666), Bar & Pub (1,623), and Bakery & Sweets (818) lead the kinds of food. 4,369 hold an on-premises liquor license from the State Liquor Authority. 1,810 are locations of a chain with five or more in New York. 55 are on our hand-checked guides; the longest at the same address is Fraunces Tavern, there since 1762.

Health inspections here

9,229 places in Manhattan have a letter grade or grade status on file with the NYC Health Department: 8,052 A, 603 B and 170 C, and 404 pending or not yet graded. Each grade is official and dated, from the Health Department's records through Sep 29, 2026; we never compute one from an inspection score. In the web search, each place's panel shows its grade, its latest inspection and a link to the Health Department's full history.

About the inspection results

N means not yet graded, Z grade pending, and P grade pending on re-opening after a closure. We never compute a grade from an inspection score.
