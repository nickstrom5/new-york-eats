Niagara Falls at a glance

Our directory counts 285 places to eat and drink in Niagara Falls. Bar & Pub (59), Coffee & Café (35), and Pizza (25) lead the kinds of food. 78 hold an on-premises liquor license from the State Liquor Authority. 64 are locations of a chain with five or more in New York. None of our 228 hand-checked guide places is in Niagara Falls yet, so the nearest ones are below.

Health inspections here

In Niagara County, the New York State Department of Health publishes each place's latest inspection: its date and the counts of critical and other violations. There is no official grade there, and we don't make one. We matched 236 of the 285 places in Niagara Falls to a state inspection record; each one's latest inspection date and counts are in its panel in the web search, with NYSDOH's own disclaimer.
