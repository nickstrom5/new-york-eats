Syracuse at a glance

Our directory counts 751 places to eat and drink in Syracuse. Bar & Pub (117), Coffee & Café (96), and Bakery & Sweets (58) lead the kinds of food. 170 hold an on-premises liquor license from the State Liquor Authority. 139 are locations of a chain with five or more in New York. 5 are on our hand-checked guides; the longest at the same address is Nibsy's Pub, there since 1890.

Health inspections here

In Onondaga County, the New York State Department of Health publishes each place's latest inspection: its date and the counts of critical and other violations. There is no official grade there, and we don't make one. We matched 655 of the 751 places in Syracuse to a state inspection record; each one's latest inspection date and counts are in its panel in the web search, with NYSDOH's own disclaimer.

About the inspection results

Outside New York City there is no official restaurant grade, and we don't make one. Where shown, the date and counts are from the New York State Department of Health's latest inspection on file (health.data.ny.gov), records through Sep 24, 2026. In NYSDOH's words:

“Any use of this data to develop a restaurant grading system is not supported or endorsed by the New York State Department of Health.”

Any analyses, interpretations or conclusions here are ours, not NYSDOH's. Places are never ordered by these counts.
