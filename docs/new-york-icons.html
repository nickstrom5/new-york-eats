Honors are facts, not ratings

Each honor is written out as it was given: "1 MICHELIN Star" from the MICHELIN Guide New York 2025 selection, "James Beard nominee 2026" from the James Beard Foundation's own announcements (2023 to 2026), "America's Classic" for the foundation's America's Classics. A nominee is never called a winner. Historic places were checked against their own history or local news, and the year is when the place opened at its current address. Every MICHELIN selection in the state is in New York City or Westchester.

The order on this page is by region and city, never by anyone's rating. The app's New York Icons list orders the same places by their honors and years at the address; those points are not a rating. Places open and close, so check before you go. MICHELIN and the MICHELIN Guide are trademarks of Michelin. James Beard Foundation and James Beard Award are trademarks of the James Beard Foundation.

About the inspection results

NYC letter grades are the NYC Health Department's own, labeled official and shown with the date of the graded inspection: A, B or C, posted at the door. N means not yet graded, Z grade pending, and P grade pending on re-opening after a closure. We never compute a grade from an inspection score. Source: NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene, NYC Open Data, records through Sep 29, 2026; the full history is on the Health Department's ABCEats site.

Outside New York City there is no official restaurant grade, and we don't make one. Where shown, the date and counts are from the New York State Department of Health's latest inspection on file (health.data.ny.gov), records through Sep 24, 2026. In NYSDOH's words:

“Any use of this data to develop a restaurant grading system is not supported or endorsed by the New York State Department of Health.”

Any analyses, interpretations or conclusions here are ours, not NYSDOH's. Places are never ordered by these counts.

Suffolk County publishes the violations its inspectors cited each year, with no score or grade; an inspection that found nothing isn't on the list. Counts shown are violations cited in 2025, the latest year published (Suffolk County Department of Health Services, opendata.suffolkcountyny.gov).

Erie County, Buffalo included, posts its restaurant inspections only through a search-only lookup, so there is no inspection data for its places here.
