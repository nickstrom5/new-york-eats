Capital Region at a glance

Our directory counts 2,998 restaurants, cafés, bars and bakeries in the Capital Region; the places with the most are Albany (454), Schenectady (253), Saratoga Springs (218), and Troy (187). Bar & Pub (578), Coffee & Café (284), Pizza (280), and Sandwiches & Deli (155) lead the kinds of food. 13 places are on our hand-checked guides, and 4 more carry a MICHELIN or James Beard honor.

Health inspections here

In the other counties, the New York State Department of Health publishes each place's latest inspection: its date and the counts of critical and other violations. There is no official grade there, and we don't make one. Albany County inspects on its own system, and only part of the results reach the state's open data, so many places there have no state record here.

About the inspection results

Outside New York City there is no official restaurant grade, and we don't make one. Where shown, the date and counts are from the New York State Department of Health's latest inspection on file (health.data.ny.gov), records through Sep 24, 2026. In NYSDOH's words:

“Any use of this data to develop a restaurant grading system is not supported or endorsed by the New York State Department of Health.”

Any analyses, interpretations or conclusions here are ours, not NYSDOH's. Places are never ordered by these counts.
