New York City at a glance

Our directory counts 30,976 restaurants, cafés, bars and bakeries in New York City; the places with the most are Manhattan (12,069), Brooklyn (8,117), Queens (6,991), and Bronx (2,640). Coffee & Café (3,737), Bar & Pub (3,200), Bakery & Sweets (2,512), and Chinese (2,275) lead the kinds of food. 92 places are on our hand-checked guides, and 222 more carry a MICHELIN or James Beard honor. 23,925 have a letter grade or grade status on file with the NYC Health Department: 20,443 A, 1,776 B and 519 C, and 1,187 pending or not yet graded (records through Sep 29, 2026).

About the inspection results

NYC letter grades are the NYC Health Department's own, labeled official and shown with the date of the graded inspection: A, B or C, posted at the door. N means not yet graded, Z grade pending, and P grade pending on re-opening after a closure. We never compute a grade from an inspection score. Source: NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene, NYC Open Data, records through Sep 29, 2026; the full history is on the Health Department's ABCEats site.
